## Common questions

**How do I study with a friend?**
Open **Partner**, add a friend by username or share your friend link, then tap **Start Focus Session** and pick them. To pair with someone just once, use **Generate Pairing Code** and have them enter it.

**How do I change how long focus and breaks last?**
Go to **Settings → Timer Durations** and tap Focus, Short Break or Long Break to pick any length. You can also adjust them for a single session when you start it with a friend.

**How does app blocking work?**
Go to **Settings → App Blocking**, allow Screen Time access, and choose the apps to block. They're blocked only during focus sessions and unblocked when you pause or stop. PomoDuo never sees which apps you choose.

**Why do I see websites in the app picker?**
Apple's picker lists websites from your iPhone's own Screen Time history. PomoDuo can't see them, and apps can't hide them.

**How do I delete my account?**
Go to **Settings → Account → Delete Account**. This removes your data from our servers.
